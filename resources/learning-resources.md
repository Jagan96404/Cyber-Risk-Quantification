# Learning Resources for Cyber Risk Quantification

A curated collection of educational materials, courses, books, academic papers, and references to help you master CRQ methodologies and frameworks.

---

## 📚 Books & Textbooks

### Foundational Texts

- **"How to Measure Anything in Cybersecurity Risk"** by Douglas W. Hubbard & Richard Seiersen
  - A practical guide to quantifying cybersecurity risks using statistical methods and Monte Carlo simulations.
  - Publisher: Wiley | ISBN: 978-1119563632

- **"Open FAIR Risk Analysis: A Handbook"** by Cynthia Dion-Schwarz
  - Comprehensive introduction to the Open FAIR methodology with practical case studies.
  - Publisher: FAIR Institute | Available as PDF

- **"Risk Management Framework"** by National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
  - Official NIST guidelines for managing information security risk.
  - URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-39/final

- **"The Failure of Risk Management"** by Douglas W. Hubbard
  - Critique of traditional risk management and advocacy for quantitative approaches.
  - Publisher: Wiley | ISBN: 978-1118122631

## 🌐 Standards & Frameworks

### Open FAIR™ Standard

- **Open FAIR Standard** (ISO/IEC 42010:2022 Aligned)
  - Open-source risk taxonomy and standard for cyber risk quantification.
  - URL: https://www.fairinstitute.org/open-fair

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**
  - Core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
  - URL: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework/

### ISO/IEC Standards

- **ISO/IEC 27005:2022** - Information Security Risk Management
  - International standard for risk management in information security.

- **ISO/IEC 31000:2018** - Risk Management - Guidelines
  - Universal framework for enterprise risk management.

## 🎯 Getting Started Path

### Recommended Learning Sequence

1. **Week 1**: Read "How to Measure Anything in Cybersecurity Risk"
2. **Week 2**: Study NIST Cybersecurity Framework fundamentals
3. **Week 3**: Take FAIR certification foundation course (FAIR Institute)
4. **Week 4**: Learn Python with pyfair or R with evaluator
5. **Ongoing**: Join FAIR Institute community and attend webinars
