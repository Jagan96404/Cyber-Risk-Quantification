# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all contributors, maintainers, and community members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Respectful Communication**: Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- **Constructive Feedback**: Providing thoughtful, actionable suggestions and critique.
- **Accountability**: Acknowledging mistakes and learning from feedback.
- **Inclusive Participation**: Encouraging and valuing diverse perspectives and experiences.
- **Professionalism**: Maintaining a professional and collegial tone in all interactions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, age, race, or religion.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information (e.g., physical or email address) without consent.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate or harmful.

---

## Enforcement

Community members who violate this Code of Conduct may face consequences determined by project maintainers, ranging from:

1. **Warning**: A private message explaining the violation and expectations for future behavior.
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Temporary restriction from contributing to the project.
3. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the project community.

---

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Report Privately**: Contact project maintainers via email or private message with:
   - Description of the incident
   - Names or usernames of those involved
   - Context and any supporting information
   - Your preferred way to be contacted

2. **Do Not Engage Publicly**: Avoid publicly confronting the violator; report to maintainers instead.

3. **Expect Confidentiality**: Reports will be handled confidentially, and no names will be published without consent.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki)
- Community events (virtual or in-person)
- Official project communications
- Any other spaces where project community activity occurs
