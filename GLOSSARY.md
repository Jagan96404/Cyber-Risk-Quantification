# Cyber Risk Quantification - Glossary of Terms

A comprehensive glossary of key terminology used in Cyber Risk Quantification (CRQ) and related disciplines.

---

## A

### ALE (Annualized Loss Expectancy)
The average dollar amount of loss an organization can expect from a specific risk over one year. Calculated as: **ALE = SLE (Single Loss Expectancy) × ARF (Annualized Rate of Occurrence)**.

### ARF (Annualized Rate of Frequency)
The estimated number of times a risk event is expected to occur in one year.

### ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)
Total predictable recurring revenue that a company expects to receive from customers in a one-year period.

### ASM (Attack Surface Management)
The process of discovering, monitoring, and analyzing all external-facing assets and potential attack vectors.

### AVARC (Asset, Vulnerability, Attacker, Risk, Control)
A cyber risk assessment framework model.

---

## B

### Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
Assessment of potential business consequences of disruptive events on critical business functions and processes.

---

## C

### C2M2 (Cybersecurity Capability Maturity Model)
A self-assessment tool by CISA for measuring and monitoring cybersecurity capabilities across organizations.

### CAPEC (Common Attack Pattern Expression and Community)
A comprehensive dictionary and classification taxonomy of known attacks that can be used by analysts, developers, testers, and educators.

### CISM (Certified Information Security Manager)
An international certification for information security managers and leaders.

### CISO (Chief Information Security Officer)
Senior executive responsible for an organization's information security strategy and implementation.

### CVE (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures)
An identifier for publicly disclosed cybersecurity vulnerabilities, maintained by MITRE and CISA.

### CWE (Common Weakness Enumeration)
A community-developed list of common software and hardware weaknesses.

### CTEM (Continuous Threat Exposure Management)
Continuous monitoring and management of external and internal threat exposures across the cyber ecosystem.

---

## E

### EASM (External Attack Surface Management)
Continuous discovery and monitoring of an organization's external-facing assets and potential attack vectors.

### EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)
A probability scoring system developed by CISA and Fortify that predicts the likelihood of a vulnerability being exploited in the wild.

---

## F

### FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk)
A standardized, open-source quantitative risk analysis framework that decomposes risk into measurable factors: **Risk = Threat Event Frequency × Loss Magnitude × Loss Exceedance**.

### FAIR-U
An educational tool and web application provided by the FAIR Institute for learning and simulating FAIR-based risk analyses.

---

## G

### GRC (Governance, Risk, Compliance)
A comprehensive strategy integrating governance, risk management, and compliance functions.

---

## I

### IRM (Integrated Risk Management)
Unified approach to managing enterprise risks across all business functions and departments.

---

## K

### KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)
A catalog maintained by CISA of vulnerabilities that are known to be actively exploited by adversaries.

---

## L

### LEC (Loss Exceedance Curve)
A graphical representation showing the probability of exceeding various loss amounts over a specific time period.

### LEV (Loss Event Frequency)
The estimated frequency of loss-causing events in a given time period.

---

## M

### Monte Carlo Simulation
A probabilistic computational technique that generates random samples to estimate the distribution of outcomes for complex systems.

---

## N

### NIST CSF (NIST Cybersecurity Framework)
A framework developed by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) providing guidelines for managing cybersecurity risk.

---

## O

### Open FAIR™
An open-source, standardized ontology and standard for quantitative risk analysis based on the FAIR model.

### OWASP (Open Web Application Security Project)
A nonprofit organization focused on improving software security through community-driven projects and resources.

---

## P

### POC (Proof of Concept)
A demonstration or trial typically used by vendors to showcase product capabilities before purchasing.

### POV (Proof of Value)
A customer engagement model where a vendor demonstrates the value of their product through a time-limited evaluation.

### PSAT (Prioritization Scoring and Assessment Tool)
Tools used to assess and prioritize security risks and controls.

---

## R

### RaC (Risk as Code)
An approach to modeling, defining, and executing cyber risk assessments programmatically using code and configuration languages.

### ROSI (Return on Security Investment)
A metric calculating the financial benefits gained from security investments relative to their costs.

### RSI™ (Ransomware Susceptibility Index)
A proprietary scoring system (by Black Kite) assessing an organization's vulnerability to ransomware attacks.

---

## S

### SLE (Single Loss Expectancy)
The estimated financial loss from a single occurrence of a risk event.

---

## T

### TEF (Threat Event Frequency)
The estimated number of times a threat event is expected to occur in a given time period.

### TPRM (Third-Party Risk Management)
The practice of identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks posed by vendors, suppliers, and other third parties.

### TVM (Threat and Vulnerability Management)
Integrated approach to identifying, prioritizing, and remediating threats and vulnerabilities.

---

## V

### VaR (Value at Risk)
A statistical measure of the maximum potential loss over a specified time period at a given confidence level (e.g., 90th percentile loss).

### Vulnerability Management
Process of identifying, prioritizing, patching, and remediating software vulnerabilities.

### VMDR (Vulnerability Management, Detection, and Response)
Integrated platform combining vulnerability scanning, threat detection, and incident response capabilities.

---

## W

### WAF (Web Application Firewall)
Security tool designed to protect web applications from attacks by filtering and monitoring HTTP/HTTPS traffic.

---

## X

### XaaS (Everything as a Service)
Broad category of cloud-based services delivered as managed, subscription-based offerings (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, etc.).

---

## Y

### YoY (Year-over-Year)
Comparison of metrics or performance between the same period in consecutive years.

---

## Z

### Zero-Trust Security
A security paradigm that requires strict verification and authentication for every access request, regardless of user or device.
